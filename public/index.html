Attribute a ransomware Bitcoin address with free tools

A source hands you a single Bitcoin address they say belongs to a ransomware affiliate. Using only free or freemium tools, walk through how you would establish who controls it and where the funds ultimately get cashed out. Name the specific tools at each step, and state clearly where this kind of attribution typically breaks down.
